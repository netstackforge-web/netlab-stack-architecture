# **Cable & Link Standards**

    - 10GbE / SFP+: DAC or fiber runs (1.5 to 3M standard DAC cables used 2X1.5M and 4X3M)

    - Point-to-Point Links: Direct host-to-storage/NVR links bypassing shared switch bottlenecks

    - Ethernet / PoE: Cat6/Cat6A runs for workstations, APs, and PoE endpoints.
  
## **Physical Placement & Power**

    Where each switch, router, AP, and server sits physically

    Node power is supplied through two power strips, splitting wattage load across two standard 15A household wall outlets

    Standalone PoE injectors have been eliminated in favor of centralized inline power delivery across two managed PoE access switches, distributing endpoint wattage within each switch's total PoE power budget.
